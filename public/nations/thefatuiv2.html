The Fatui

Overview & Short History:

Nod Krai, also known as the Borderlands, is an autonomous region of Snezhnaya, located at the southernmost area of their land and at the very edge of Teyvat. It has no ruling archon, and instead the people of the Frostmoon Scions worship the Moon Maiden they consider to be their Goddess. Nod Krai’s land is made up of remnants of a moon that failed to form properly and instead fell into the oceans, eventually turning into islands with harsh environments which the people found their way around. Some natives of Nod Krai have special abilities gained from using a special power called kuuvahki that is drawn from the moon, a primordial power that predates even the seven elements of Teyvat. Nod Krai, due to its location near the edge of Teyvat, was unfortunately one of the first nations to be attacked by the Abyss after the Cataclysm of Khaenriah.
