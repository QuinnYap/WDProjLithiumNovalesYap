The Fatui

Overview & Short History:

The Fatui is a powerful organization based in Snezhnaya, one of the seven nations in Teyvat. They are known for their influence and power, often engaging in political and economic activities to further their goals. The Fatui is led by the Tsaritsa, the Cryo Archon of Snezhnaya, who is one of the seven archons that rule over the nations of Teyvat. The organization is known for its ruthless tactics and willingness to do whatever it takes to achieve their objectives, often resorting to manipulation, espionage, and even violence. The Fatui has a significant presence in the world of Genshin Impact, with various characters and storylines revolving around their actions and influence.

A group of 11 extremely talented individuals known as the Fatui Harbingers belong to this organization, each with their own unique abilities and roles within it. They are ranked based on their strength and abilities. The top three harbingers are said to possess powers that are comparable to that of gods.

The Traveler's first bump-in with a Fatui Harbinger was with La Signora the "Fair Lady", eighth of the Fatui Harbingers. She made her first appearance in Act III of the Prologue, where she restrained the Traveler and also kicked the undercover Anemo Archon, which set a bad impression on the Traveler towards the organization she belongs to, the Fatui.

The 11 Fatui Harbingers:

Playable:

Columbina "The Damselette", Arlecchino "The Knave", Scaramouche "The Balladeer", Tartaglia "Childe"

Non-Playable:

Capitano "The Captain", Dottore "The Doctor", Pulcinella "The Rooster", Sandrone "Marionette", Signora "The Fair Lady", Pantalone "Regrator", Unknown (not yet introduced)
